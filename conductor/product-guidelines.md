# Product Guidelines: Block Puzzle 10x10

## 1. Visual Identity
**Default Style: Classic Arcade**
- **Aesthetic:** Bold, readable, and retro-inspired but polished for modern high-resolution screens.
- **Shapes:** Rounded square grid cells and pieces to soften the "Arcade" look while maintaining clarity.
- **Theming:** The visual system must be themeable. Users can switch between different visual styles (e.g., Arcade, Minimalist, Cyberpunk).
- **Default Palette:** A "Retro RGB" or high-contrast palette against a dark background, ensuring high visibility for pieces. Colors are cosmetic but must be distinct.

## 2. Voice & Tone
**Dynamic & Adaptive**
The game's communication style shifts based on the player's context:
- **Functional (Standard Gameplay):** Concise and clear updates (e.g., "+10", "Level Up") to avoid distracting from the puzzle.
- **Encouraging (Steady Progress):** Supportive feedback during sustained play (e.g., "Nice move", "Keep it up").
- **High-Energy (Moments of Brilliance):** Enthusiastic and celebratory text for combos, multi-line clears, and high scores (e.g., "UNSTOPPABLE!", "PERFECT!", "NEW RECORD!").

## 3. Motion & Interaction Design
**Hybrid Animation Strategy**
- **Gameplay (Snappy):** Piece pickup, dragging, and placement must be instant and responsive. Zero input lag.
- **UI & Feedback (Smooth):** Screen transitions, score counting, and menu overlays should use fluid ease-in/ease-out curves to feel premium and polished.
- **Feedback Loops:** Visual cues (highlighting valid spots, dimming invalid ones) should be immediate.

## 4. User Personalization
**Configurability First**
- **Themes:** Users can change the entire visual theme (colors, background, block styles).
- **Haptics:** Users can customize haptic feedback intensity (e.g., Off, Light, Heavy). The default will be "Tactile & Detailed" to provide rich feedback for actions like dragging, placing, and clearing.
- **Audio:** Separate controls for SFX and Music (if implemented in the future).

## 5. UI/UX Principles
- **Clarity:** The board state and available pieces are the heroes. UI elements (score, power-ups) are secondary but easily accessible.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure sufficient contrast ratios in all themes.
- **Dark Mode:** The game defaults to a Dark Theme (as per product specs) to reduce eye strain and save battery on OLED screens.
